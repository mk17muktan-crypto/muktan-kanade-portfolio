Dagdushet Ganpati Design Solution

Who is the client?

Dagdushet Ganpati is a religious and devotional organisation with a strong emotional connection among devotees, visitors and the wider community.

What was the design need?

The project needed a clear and consistent visual communication system that could support festival campaigns, social media creatives and devotional brand communication.

What kind of audience was this for?

The audience included devotees, families, festival visitors, social media followers and people who connect with the brand through cultural and spiritual content.

What emotion should the design create?

The design needed to feel devotional, warm, trustworthy and celebratory while maintaining visual clarity across different digital and campaign formats.
