About me

I’m a Visual Communication Designer with 3.5 years of agency experience, turning complex brand, product and campaign ideas into clear, consistent and platform-ready visual communication.

At Indian Magic Eye, I work across brand identity, campaign design, social media creatives, digital design, print design, UI/UX layouts, motion graphics, reels, packaging, brochures, hoardings, websites, app interfaces and AI-assisted creative workflows. My client exposure spans chemicals, construction, education, engineering, fashion, food, healthcare, hospitality, politics, networking, pumps and water, religious organisations, theatre, entertainment, stationery, CSR and mobility.

My work usually starts with understanding the brief, audience, brand language and communication objective. From there, I develop visual directions, create layouts, maintain brand consistency and execute final design assets across multiple formats and platforms.

In the absence of a senior designer for nearly two years, I have taken ownership of creative execution, supported fellow designers with software and production challenges, and helped campaigns move from concept to delivery within tight timelines.
