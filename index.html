About me

I’m a Visual Communication Designer with over 3.5 years of agency experience at Indian Magic Eye.

My work focuses on turning brand, product and campaign ideas into clear, consistent and platform-ready visual communication. I work across digital design, graphic design, UI layouts, print design, motion content and AI-assisted creative workflows.

My design process starts with understanding the brief, audience, brand language and communication objective. From there, I build visual directions, create layouts & execute design assets that are practical & ready for real-world use.

Over the last two years, I have also taken greater ownership of creative execution, working through tight timelines, supporting fellow designers and helping projects move from concept to final delivery.
