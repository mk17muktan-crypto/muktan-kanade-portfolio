About me

I’m a Visual Communication Designer with over 3.5 years of agency experience.

My work focuses on turning ideas into clear, consistent & platform-ready visual communication. I have worked across many mediums such as: Graphic Design, Ui/Ux Design, Print Design, Motion Graphics, Video Editing & I have incorporated the latest AI Image Generation & Video Generation tools in my workflow such as ChatGPT, Google Gemini, Midjourney & other Adobe Ai tools.

My design process starts with understanding the brief, audience, brand language and communication objective. From there, I build visual directions, create layouts & execute design assets that are practical & ready for real-world use.

Over the last two years, I have also taken greater ownership of creative execution, working through tight timelines, supporting fellow designers & helping projects move from concept to final delivery.
